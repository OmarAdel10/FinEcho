## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial atmosphere for offline-first personal financial management. Merging the calm discipline of Swiss print typography with the tactile poise of contemporary AI research interfaces, it treats personal ledgering not as a noisy transactional chore, but as a deliberate ritual of clarity.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Sovereign & Quiet:** Rejects gamified animations, loud gradients, and hyperactive alerts. Financial status is communicated with the gravity and restraint of a bespoke portfolio folio.
- **Architectural & Exact:** Layouts rely on strict typographic tension, disciplined micro-rules, and balanced tonal fields rather than decorative illustration.
- **Immediate & Local-First:** Communicates rock-solid stability. UI states feel grounded, immediate, and free from cloud latency or sync anxiety.

### Design Movement: Modern Editorial Minimalism
The interface leverages sweeping fields of stark white canvas juxtaposed against dense, ink-saturated deep green and midnight fields. Content surfaces rest on subtle mineral washes, framed with surgical hairline rules. Structural rhythm is governed by strong vertical proportion, compact uppercase display labeling, and razor-sharp monospaced data fidelity.

## Colors

The palette uses high-contrast ink values paired with muted mineral planes and strategic precision accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure optical white base providing maximum negative space and crisp contrast.
- **Primary Ink (`#003C33`):** Deep botanical green-black used for primary brand moments, high-impact account hero panels, and focused interactions.
- **Secondary Ink (`#071829`):** Deep cold navy utilized for secondary ledger sections, deep metric backdrops, and navigation chrome.
- **Text & Core Neutral (`#17171C`):** High-density carbon ink for authoritative typographic hierarchy.
- **Mineral Surface (`#EEECE7`):** Warm, soft stone neutral used for grouped inset containers, discrete segmented controls, and secondary card surfaces.
- **Positive / Inflow Accent (`#EDFCE9`):** Subdued pale green surface highlight used for credit badges, inflow indicators, and verified offline sync badges.
- **Interactive Blue (`#1863DC`):** Crisp precision azure reserved exclusively for interactive links, selectable range trackers, and active system signals.
- **Warning / Outflow Coral (`#FF7759`):** Controlled coral accent reserved for burn warnings, debt indicators, and pending reconciliations.
- **Structural Hairline (`#D9D9DD`):** Surgical border tone used strictly at 1px thickness for ledger rows, card perimeters, and navigational rules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces a disciplined contrast between editorial sans-serif headers, unyielding body copy, and tabular monospaced ledger numerals.

### Typographic Rules
- **Display Sans (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Cut with tight tracking, sharp ascenders, and geometric clarity. Always typeset with negative letter-spacing for headlines and totals.
- **Interface Body (`Inter`):** Workhorse for functional labels, descriptions, and metadata. Provides neutral clarity at small scales.
- **Tabular Mono (`JetBrains Mono`):** Non-negotiable for all balances, transaction lines, timestamps, and status IDs. Ensures columns align down the decimal point across vertical feeds without shifts.
- **Section Headers:** Use `label-caps` in uppercase styling with `0.06em` letter spacing for ledger categories and date headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a standard 8pt mathematical rhythm optimized for mobile density and fluid touch response.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Mobile Foundation:** Fluid 4-column layout with fixed `16px` (`1rem`) lateral canvas margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Element flows follow strict multiples of 4px and 8px (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`).
- **Touch Bounds:** Touch targets adhere to a minimum bounding box of 44×44px, regardless of the visible geometry of the internal pill or icon.
- **Safe Zones:** Top header areas accommodate device status notches using a standard 56px app bar height with 16px bottom pad. Bottom sheets and navigation bars respect the safe inset area + 8px elevation padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects heavy drop shadows in favor of tonal surface tiers, deliberate color blocks, and ultra-minimal razor outlines.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Surface Inset (Level 1):** Solid `#EEECE7` (mineral stone) used to define modules, balance charts, and categorical groupings without drop shadows.
- **High-Density Focus (Level 2 - Deep Bands):** Inverted product bands in `#003C33` (deep green) and `#071829` (dark navy). These ground the primary net-worth summaries and anchor view transitions.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (Level 3):** Grounded on `#FFFFFF` or `#EEECE7` backed by an ultra-crisp hairline rule (`1px solid #D9D9DD`) accompanied by a subtle ambient razor blur: `0px 4px 16px rgba(23, 23, 28, 0.04)`.
- **Dividers & Delimiters:** Transaction list separation uses pure `1px` hairlines (`#D9D9DD`) with zero gradient falloff.

## Shapes

The geometric identity contrasts rounded card surfaces with pill-shaped control elements.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Master Cards & Media Panels:** Bound between `16px` and `22px` radii (`rounded-lg` through `rounded-xl`). High-mass containers (such as the total balance module) use `20px`.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Full pill execution (`rounded-full` / 32px height minimum) for action buttons, filter tags, and state pills.
- **Input Fields & Inset Controls:** Grounded at `12px` to sit comfortably inside the `20px` parent cards.
- **Corner Smoothing:** Use iOS-standard continuous squircle curvature for all card perimeters to preserve organic edge flow.

## Components

### Net-Worth Hero Cards
- **Structure:** Solid `#003C33` (deep green) or `#071829` (navy) block with 20px rounded corners.
- **Content:** Label typeset in `label-caps` (`#EDFCE9` at 80% opacity), balance figure in `numeral-hero` (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Metrics Sub-bar:** Inset stone rule separating secondary delta values (e.g., `+2.4% this month`) in `numeral-sm`.

### Transaction Ledger Rows
- **Layout:** Horizontal split, height 56px, bordered bottom by 1px `#D9D9DD` hairline.
- **Leading Element:** Category glyph or 32px square neutral icon block (`#EEECE7`, 8px radius).
- **Center Element:** Payee title in `title-md` (`#17171C`), sub-row category/time in `body-sm`.
- **Trailing Element:** Numerical value right-aligned using `numeral-lg`. Debits set in `#17171C`; credits set in `#003C33`.

### Action Buttons & CTA Elements
- **Primary Pill:** 32px to 40px height, 9999px radius (pill). Background `#17171C` with `#FFFFFF` text or `#003C33` with `#EDFCE9` text. Font: `label-caps`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill:** 1px hairline border in `#D9D9DD`, transparent fill, `#17171C` text.
- **Active State:** On press, scale instantly to `0.98` with background opacity shifting to 90%.

### Confidence & Status Badges
- **Local-Only Sync Indicator:** Pill container with `#EDFCE9` background, 1px `#D9D9DD` border, `#003C33` text in `label-status`. Prefixed with a 6px solid green status circle.
- **Pending / Coral Tag:** Subdued `#FF7759` tint background (12% opacity), text in `#FF7759`, typeset in `label-status`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Surface:** `#EEECE7` stone wash, no border by default. 12px corner radius. Minimum height 48px.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#1863DC` hairline halo.
- **Typography:** Value input in `numeral-lg` for monetary values, `body-lg` for descriptions. Placeholder color: `#17171C` at 40% opacity.
